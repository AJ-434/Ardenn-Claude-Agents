### Anticipated Questions & Answers

**Q: Why not just hire a UK sales team?**
A: Field sales would cost £500K+ and take 18-24 months to cover the market. Digital provides broader reach at lower CAC and builds permanent assets.

**Q: What if results are slower than projected?**
A: We have 90-day review gates and paid search as a hedge. We can pivot based on data. However, 12-18 months is industry-standard for SEO maturity.

**Q: How do we compete with established brands like Sebo?**
A: We're not competing across their full line—we're owning the backpack vacuum specialist position where they're weak. Niche focus beats generalist.

**Q: What's our distributor strategy?**
A: Digital drives demand, distributors fulfill. We're recruiting in parallel. Our "where to buy" presence makes us valuable to distributors.

**Q: Can we do this cheaper/faster?**
A: Cheaper = cutting corners that hurt results. Faster = paid advertising only (no lasting assets, higher CAC). This is optimized for sustainable growth.

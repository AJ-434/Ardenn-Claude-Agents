# Pacvac UK SEO Strategy & Market Growth Plan

## Executive Summary

This document presents a comprehensive SEO strategy for Pacvac to grow their presence in the UK commercial cleaning equipment market. Based on extensive market research, this strategy identifies key opportunities in a £166 billion cleaning services industry and provides actionable recommendations for market penetration.

   - Antimicrobial coatings market projected at $20.1 billion by 2026
